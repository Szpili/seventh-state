The part most systems get wrong

Suppose this was marked True on the strength of one document — and that document is later exposed as a forgery. Watch where it goes.

Two axes, never one score

Truth is about the world. Framing is about whoever wrote it. Merge them into a single rating and "it feels right" quietly becomes "it is right".

Green is a claim, not a fact

Green means "I declare no spin" — not "this is objective". Nobody can certify their own neutrality, so it's disputable like the other two.

Evidence can die

When a claim loses its evidence it doesn't become false — nobody showed the opposite. It becomes unsupported and returns to "I don't know".
